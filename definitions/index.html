The platform

Where agents live, what they do, and what they need.

Studio

What agents need

Studio is the governed toolkit behind every agent. Bring together integrations, interface components, design systems, skills and prompts, then manage them once for every team and agent. Build faster, stay on brand and improve what your agents can do without rebuilding every experience.

Learn more→

Canvas

What agents do

Canvas is where teams design, test and run agents as visible workflows. Connect knowledge, models, tools and approvals, inspect every step, then move from prototype to production with confidence. Complex agent behaviour becomes understandable, collaborative and controllable.

Learn more→

Spatial

Where agents live

Spatial turns everything your business knows into a living context layer that agents can navigate. It connects products, customers, content, policies and relationships so every agent understands what matters, for whom and when. The result is more relevant experiences and decisions grounded in how your business actually works.
